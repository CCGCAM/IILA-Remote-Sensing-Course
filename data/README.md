# Supplied data for the course

`Achterhoek_FieldSpec_2008.csv` contains 40 plot spectra from 350–2500 nm. `Achterhoek_FieldSpec_2008_field_sampling.csv` contains the matching plot IDs and field measurements: fresh and dry biomass, nitrogen concentration and nitrogen content. Exercise 3 uses these files to explore index relationships and compare field spectra with a new random PROSAIL LUT. The LUT is generated during the practical with a fixed seed for reproducibility; students do not need to save it.

`soil_spectrum_bablet_ID1.csv`, `soil_spectrum_humper_ID17.csv` and `pixel_data.csv` were copied from this repository's `AEO-Course/Tables` for portable classroom use. The first two are measured soil reflectance examples; Exercise 1 plots the Bablet spectrum next to the package's default dry and wet soil. Bablet covers 400–2400 nm and Humper covers 400–2298 nm, so neither is silently substituted for ToolsRTM's full 400–2500 nm soil vector. `pixel_data.csv` has nine real Sentinel-2 profiles with 10 surface-reflectance bands, SCL and NDVI. Their coordinates are near 49.73°N, 5.43°E. This file is retained as an optional example and is not used in the main Exercise 3.

`stac_seed_marcos_juarez.csv` is a **real** Sentinel-2 Collection 1 Level-2A point time series. It was read from the [Element 84 Earth Search STAC API](https://earth-search.aws.element84.com/v1), collection `sentinel-2-c1-l2a`, on 26 November 2026. The demonstration point is longitude -62.12, latitude -32.71, near Marcos Juárez, Córdoba, Argentina. The query covers 1 October 2024 to 31 March 2025, with scene cloud cover at most 25%. The helper takes the least cloudy item in each 14-day interval, reads the point from ten surface-reflectance assets and the SCL asset, and keeps SCL 4 (vegetation) or 5 (bare soil). Thirteen dates remain. The `item_id` column identifies each STAC record.

The COG reflectance asset scale and offset are applied by `terra` during extraction. The CSV values are surface reflectance, not raw digital numbers. `NDVI`, `NDRE` and `NDMI` are calculated from the listed band values. `R/stac_series.R` repeats the search and extraction when a connection is available. STAC access is optional during class; the saved CSV is the default.

This is one pixel for a teaching example. It is not a field boundary, crop identification or independent validation of RTM trait retrieval.
